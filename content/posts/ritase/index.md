## Project Scope & Key Features
- Transportation Management System (Shipper App): SaaS platform for shippers to create shipment orders, manage bookings, and track deliveries
- Fleet Management System (Transporter App): SaaS platform for transporters to manage vehicles, assign drivers, and fulfill shipment orders
- Driver App: mobile app for drivers to receive trip assignments, update delivery status, and navigate routes
- Internal Dashboard: back-office panel for monitoring operations, managing users, and overseeing transactions across the platform
- Rit2Go B2C Platform: retail interface for end users to directly access products offered on the platform
- Ritshop: marketplace module for purchasing goods or supplies within the ecosystem
- Ritase Merchant App: app for merchants to manage their listings, orders, and store operations
